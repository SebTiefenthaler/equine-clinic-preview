One case, told properly

A horse, a decision,
an outcome.

One case written up in full, instead of six short quotes. What went wrong, what the imaging showed, what was decided, and what it took to get back to work.

A nine-year-old warmblood show jumper came in with a right forelimb lameness that had been building for weeks and had started costing him his rounds. Imaging found a focal osteochondral lesion in the fetlock. It was treated arthroscopically, and follow-up radiographs at twelve weeks showed the joint healing as expected.

Horse

Presented
