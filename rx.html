Compounding

When the licensed product
does not fit the horse.

A compounded medicine is made for one patient, to a prescription. It is mixed because a horse needs a strength, a form or a flavour that no manufacturer makes.

Strength
A dose that suits a 380 kg pony or a 700 kg warmblood, instead of whatever the box contains.
Form
Paste, powder, solution or gel, whichever one goes into the horse twice a day without a fight.
Flavour
Apple, molasses, peppermint. If the horse refuses it, the treatment stops.
Combination
Two actives in one syringe where the prescriber wants them together.

The rules

Said plainly,
once.

Four rules decide whether your order can be filled today. Two minutes here saves a phone call later.

A prescription needs a real relationship

A veterinarian has to have examined your horse and be responsible for its care. That is the law, not our policy, and it is why we ask who your vet is before we ask what you need.

Controlled substances are different

They cannot be refilled on a phone call, cannot be posted the way everything else is, and are recorded every time they move. Plan them ahead of a show or a haul.

Cold chain does not come back

Anything refrigerated, vaccines and biologics included, cannot be returned once it leaves us, because we cannot vouch for how it travelled. Check the order before it ships.

Compounded means made for your horse

It is mixed against one prescription, so it cannot be returned, and it keeps for less time than a sealed product. The label carries the date that matters.
